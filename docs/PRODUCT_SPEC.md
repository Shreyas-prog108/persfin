# Product spec

Hisab helps an Indian student, salaried person, or family answer a money question without waiting for an adviser to explain the arithmetic.

## Promise

The number on the page is the output of a named formula and the inputs on the page. A return, a tax slab, and a product term are either a versioned rule or a value the user can see and edit.

## Who it is for

People comparing an FD with a SIP, estimating take-home pay, or turning a wedding, education bill, or retirement expense into a monthly amount.

## What they can do without an account

- Search calculators by a plain question.
- Change inputs and see a new result without a reload.
- Read the formula, the assumptions, and the limits.
- Copy a link, a summary, or a result card.
- Save several goals in the browser and see which ones a monthly budget covers.
- Read a guide and open the calculator it describes.

## What the product refuses to do

- Invent a current FD rate, fund return, expense ratio, or card benefit.
- Call a coupon rate a yield.
- Present one retirement path as a guarantee.
- Recommend an insurance policy.
- Require registration before the first useful result.

## Offer, distribution, and money

The free core is the calculator and the explanation. Distribution is the guide, the shareable result, and the internal link between them. Paid ideas such as an API or an embed are out of scope until the free tools are the reason someone returns. No partnership or price is claimed in the interface.
